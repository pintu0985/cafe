ABOUT FAIRY LIGHTS HOUSE

Our Story

A little place with a lot of magic in the heart of Ajmer.

HOW IT STARTED

A Dream Illuminated by Warm Lights

Fairy Lights House Cafe was born from a simple yet heartfelt idea: to build a tranquil, glowing escape in Ajmer where friends, couples, and families could slow down, savor artisan food, and connect in an atmosphere that feels comforting and intimate.

Every corner of our cafe is thoughtfully designed with warm golden illumination, natural wooden textures, lush greenery, and soothing acoustics. We believe that dining out should be more than just eating a meal — it should be an enchanting sensory experience.

THE PHILOSOPHY

The Fairy Lights Experience

We craft every beverage and dish with intentionality. From the aromatic extraction of our freshly ground espresso to the crisp crust of our wood-fire inspired pizzas and the velvety indulgence of our molten chocolate desserts.

Whether you are stopping by for a quiet evening coffee with a book, planning a romantic dinner date, or celebrating a birthday with your closest friends, our team strives to make your evening warm, seamless, and memorable.
